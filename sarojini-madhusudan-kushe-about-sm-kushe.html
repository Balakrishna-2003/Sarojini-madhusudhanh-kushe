Our Heritage

A Legacy of Excellence & Holistic Growth

Sarojini Madhusudan Kushe Educational Institution is established under the aegis of the Sarojini Madhusudan Kushe Educational Trust, which functions as a part of the P.V.S. Memorial Charitable Trust, Mangaluru.

The institution was founded in the year 2000 by Late Sri Madhusudhana Kushe, the then Chairman of the P.V.S. Group of Companies, with a vision to provide quality education and foster the holistic development of young learners. The institution was graciously blessed and inaugurated by His Holiness Jagadguru Sri Sri Bharathi Tirtha Mahasannidhanam of Sri Sringeri Sharada Peetham, marking a significant milestone in its establishment.

The activities of the Trust are presently spearheaded by its Managing Trustee, Smt. Sarojini Madhusudan Kushe, with the active support and cooperation of the other members of the Trust. Under their guidance and stewardship, the institution continues to pursue its commitment to educational excellence and institutional development. Prof. Sainath Malligemadu, the Principal, plays a pivotal role in the academic, administrative, and developmental activities of the institution, contributing to its sustained progress.

Sarojini Madhusudan Kushe Educational Institution is a co-educational, unaided institution offering education from Lower Kindergarten (L.K.G.) to the Pre-University Course (PUC), in accordance with the curriculum prescribed by the Government of Karnataka. The institution is committed to providing a conducive learning environment that promotes academic excellence, character formation, discipline, and the holistic development of every student.

Our Guiding Principles

Motto, Vision & Purpose

Our Vision

To provide quality education that blends academic excellence with moral values, discipline, creativity, leadership, and holistic development. We strive to create a stimulating learning environment where students are encouraged to think independently, discover their talents, embrace innovation, and face future challenges with confidence.

Knowledge Through Education

Born to Be Great

We believe that education is the foundation of knowledge, character, and lifelong success. Guided by our motto, we are committed to nurturing every student's potential and empowering them to become confident, responsible, and compassionate individuals.

Our Purpose

Our belief is simple: Every child has the potential to achieve greatness, and the right education provides the path to realise it. We aim to inspire young minds to dream big, achieve their goals, and contribute meaningfully to society.

Nurturing minds, building brighter futures through quality education, strong values and lifelong learning.
