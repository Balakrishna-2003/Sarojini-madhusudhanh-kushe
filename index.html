Our Introduction

A place to learn, grow and belong.

Sarojini Madhusudan Kushe Educational Institution is committed to providing quality education in a caring, supportive and inspiring environment. The institution, sponsored by the Sarojini Madhusudan Kushe Educational Trust, offers education from Pre-K.G. through Undergraduate Course and follows the Karnataka government curriculum.

Along with academics, students are encouraged to explore project work, cultural activities, excursions and competitions, helping them develop confidence, curiosity and a broader outlook.
