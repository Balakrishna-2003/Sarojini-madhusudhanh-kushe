News & Events

Academic and Programme Report covering the vibrant activities, achievements, and milestones of our institution.

Campus Life

October 2026

Gandhi Jayanti Celebration

The birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi was celebrated with tributes and cultural programmes.

Teachers’ Training Programme

A 3-day programme covering AI and Empowerment by Mr. Nishanth Shetty B., Leadership Excellence by Mrs. Deepa Nayak P., and Effective Classroom Communication by Mrs. Vidya Shenoy.

VIA Road Safety Training

Conducted for ~20 students of Classes VIII & IX. Guests included Mr. Dayanand, Ms. Kaveri, Mr. Kishore Attavar, Mr. Raghuveer, and Mr. Shaliet to provide road safety information.

September 2026

Teachers’ Day Celebration

The programme was organised to honour teachers and acknowledge their valuable contribution to education.

Kannada Cultural Programme

The Kannada and Sanskriti Department conducted various cultural programmes to promote Kannada language, culture and traditions.

Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) Meeting

A PTA meeting was conducted to discuss students’ academic progress and strengthen coordination between parents and teachers.

Hindi Diwas

The programme was organised with Captain Prof. Dr. Sukanya Martis, former Principal of Poornaprajna Evening College, Udupi, as the guest.

Summative Assessment I (SA-I)

The SA-I examinations were conducted for school and high school students to assess their academic progress.

August 2026

Independence Day Celebration

The programme was celebrated with Sri Narayana Rai B. K., former EME personnel, Corps of the Indian Army, as the Chief Guest.

World Organ Donation Day

An awareness programme was conducted by Milagres College of Nursing. The resource person was Mr. Vishwanatha Gowda.

Muddu Krishna Competition

The competition was conducted for students from Pre-KG to Grade 4. The judges were Smt. Archana Ravi Prasad and Vidushi Mayuri S. Rao.

Bhajan Programme

A bhajan programme was conducted by our PUC students at PVS.

Onam Celebration

Onam was celebrated for our SSLC and PUC students.

Raksha Bandhan Celebration

The festival was celebrated to promote cultural values and strengthen the spirit of togetherness among students.

Sanskrit Diwas

The programme was conducted with Mr. Thirumalesh Rao, Sanskrit Guest Lecturer at Mangalore University, as the guest.

November 2025

Kannada Rajyotsava

Celebrating the formation of Karnataka state, honoring the rich Kannada language, culture, and traditions.

Cluster Level Prathiba Karanji

The competition was organised in the school. Shri Vishwanath, Education coordinator, was the Chief Guest.

Children's Day Celebration

Celebrating the joy of childhood with various fun activities, cultural programs, and special tributes to Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru.

June 2025

World Environment Day

Awareness programs, tree plantation drives, and eco-friendly initiatives were conducted by our students and staff.

Nurturing minds, building brighter futures through quality education, strong values and lifelong learning.
