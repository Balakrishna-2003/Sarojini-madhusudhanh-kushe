What We Offer

Campus Highlights & Amenities

Spacious Infrastructure

The College has good infrastructure featuring spacious classrooms, dedicated staff rooms, and well-equipped office rooms to ensure a comfortable working environment.

Silver Jubilee Celebration

We proudly celebrated our Silver Jubilee in 2025, marking 25 years of dedicated service and excellence in the field of education.

Expert Faculty

Our institution boasts highly experienced and well-qualified teaching staff who are dedicated to bringing out the best in every student.

Exemplary Discipline

The standard of discipline maintained within the campus is exemplary, creating a focused, respectful, and safe atmosphere for learning.

Seamless Connectivity

All the departments across the campus are seamlessly connected by an internal intercom system for quick communication.

Hygienic Canteen

The College features a spacious and good canteen offering hygienic and nutritious food options for students and staff to refresh themselves.

Prime Location

Located in the heart of the city, our campus is easily accessible. It is situated close to the Railway Station and the Central City Bus Stand.

Upgraded Computer Lab

The existing Computer Lab and facilities have been upgraded recently. The College now has 50 PCs specifically for the use of computer courses.

Project-Based Learning

Students are actively encouraged to take up project works in lieu of regular subjects, promoting practical and research-oriented learning.

Annual Excursions

Excursions are an annualized and much-awaited feature of our co-curricular activities, giving students a break to explore and bond.

Competitive Edge

We motivate and encourage students to actively participate in inter-college and school-level competitions to build confidence and sportsmanship.

Cultural Development

Special emphasis is laid on training students in various cultural activities, ensuring they stay connected to their roots and heritage.

Nurturing minds, building brighter futures through quality education, strong values and lifelong learning.
